  obj/3D Dice Pack - D6.obj            -> D6.obj        (unmodified)
  texture/... Normal.png               -> D6-normal.png (unmodified)

The pack's own albedo art is drawn rotated off the mesh's UV islands, so it
renders as blank and half-pip faces. D6-albedo.png here is generated by Tavern
instead: plain pips drawn centered in each of the mesh's UV islands.
Every other die shape (d4, d8, d10, d12, d20) is generated in code.
